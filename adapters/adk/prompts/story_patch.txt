INVEST Assessment Rubric & Operational Semantics:
INVEST assessments are advisory model recommendations provided as evidence for human review, subject to human discretion. Every dimension (independent, negotiable, valuable, estimable, small, testable) requires:
- result: "pass", "concern", or "fail"
- rationale: concise non-blank explanation of the rating
- evidence: concrete non-blank citation of story text, criteria, or specification grounding

Dimension Definitions & Criteria:
- independent: Delivers self-contained value without hard implementation coupling on sibling stories (pass: self-contained; concern: minor overlap; fail: hard blocker on unreleased story).
- negotiable: Captures intent/constraints without prescribing internal code design (pass: flexible implementation; concern: minor prescriptive detail; fail: dictates code/schema routines).
- valuable: Delivers observable user/stakeholder value grounded in Specification items (pass: direct grounded value; concern: indirect/enablement; fail: technical chore/no value).
- estimable: Bounded scope and clear criteria enabling confident sizing (pass: clear boundaries; concern: minor ambiguity; fail: unbounded scope/unknowns).
- small: Single focused vertical slice with bounded scenario scope (pass: focused vertical slice; concern: broad multi-flow scope needing future split; fail: unbounded composite or epic-sized scope).
- testable: Verifiable criteria with deterministic pass/fail outcomes (pass: concrete observable criteria; concern: abstract phrasing; fail: missing or untestable).

Planning Proposal Semantics (Sizing, Ordering, Dependencies):
Planning metadata values are advisory model recommendations provided as evidence for human review:
- estimated_effort: Proposed sizing estimate (XS: 1 point, S: 2 points, M: 3 points, L: 5 points, XL: 8 points) reflecting Developer sizing responsibility.
- Backlog ordering / rank: The corrected story maintains its parent Backlog position and rank upon acceptance.
- dependency_candidates: Proposed prerequisite dependencies on sibling stories or components with explicit/inferred confidence and reason.
Human acceptance binds the exact visible package before operational activation.
